data (wiki)” means precisely that no gate was found in the pinned PoB data

`PobSession.set_items()`.

## PoB-Known, Harness-Unreachable Backlog

- No public authoring/search surface exists for essences, unveiling, fractures,
  influence selection, eldritch implicits, synthesis, corruption, enchantment,
  anointment, catalyst category, beastcrafts, Delve, Crucible, Scourge,
  Necropolis, Foulborn, or Runegrafts. A valid raw item can often be evaluated,
  but raw-item import is not an optimizer search space.
- The worker can author regular/abyss socketed jewels, cluster jewels, and
  tattoo overrides through the `set_tree(extra=...)` payload. `PobSession` has
  no first-class methods for those fields, and the pipeline neither generates
  them nor assigns their tree sockets.
- Timeless jewels are preserved by `load_xml()` and `convert_tree()`, but no
  direct seed/socket API exists.
- Fixed flasks, tinctures, and request jewels are carried by skeletons, but no
  pipeline stage chooses or rolls them. Spectre selection is likewise absent.
- `Data/ModMap.lua` has no session setter; the scenario is not a loadout.

## Gates Missing From Data

- Base-item records expose `req.level`, not `dropLevel`: a base drop-level gate
  is **not in data (wiki)**.
- Drop locations, boss/league access, atlas tier, trading availability, and
  crafting-currency acquisition are generally **not in data (wiki)**. PoB's
  unique `availability` source class is the exception recorded in the unique
  row, not a universal economic gate.
- Oil-to-passive recipes, catalyst category effect/application rules,
  fracture acquisition, corruption outcomes, influence-item acquisition,
  Eldritch currency/tier rules, and special crafting composition rules are
  **not in data (wiki)** unless a future extracted record proves otherwise.
- The data has no direct availability gate for tattoos, timeless seeds,
  charms, grafts, Runegrafts, Foulborn, Scourge, Crucible, or Necropolis;
  their fields constrain how PoB calculates a supplied instance instead.
