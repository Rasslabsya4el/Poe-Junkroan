## 4. Statements about reshuffling and tier intent

No GGG statement was found that says bench-recipe maps or Pantheon maps are deliberately kept at similar tiers, or that defines a general recipe-relocation rule when a recipe map leaves the atlas.

A sourced community finding does address Pantheon reassignment:

> “The map bosses you are required to defeat to upgrade your Pantheon are mostly shuffled with new map series that released in each new league since this patch.” — PoE Wiki contributors, version-history entry for 3.5.0 (2018-12-07), no individual author shown, [Soul of Garukhan](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Soul_of_Garukhan).

The same page’s conditions attach this to a version-history note and explicitly labels it:

> “(Not in patch note)” — PoE Wiki contributors, 3.5.0 version-history entry, [same source](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Soul_of_Garukhan).

No conflicting contemporary statement was found; the dated atlas tables above show actual changed capture assignments and map tiers.

what it says: A community source reports Pantheon boss targets being shuffled with map series; no source found establishes a deliberate same-tier policy or a universal bench-recipe replacement rule.
