# Brief: where crafting-bench recipes and Pantheon powers are unlocked, and how stable that is over five leagues

Report to `docs/research/2026-09-19-bench-and-pantheon-unlocks.md`. Path of Exile 1, leagues 3.25, 3.26, 3.27, 3.28
and 3.29 (the current one). Every row needs a source URL (poewiki.net, poedb.tw, pathofexile.com patch notes or
forum, reddit.com) and, where a claim is not a plain table cell, a short verbatim quote. "Not found" is allowed and
better than a guess. No advice, no build talk: tables of facts.

Context: a build planner produces loadouts for stages of a league -- "day-1" (white and yellow maps up to tier 10),
"T16" (red maps up to tier 16) and "endgame". It must know which crafting-bench recipes and which Pantheon powers a
character can realistically have at each stage. Both are unlocked at fixed places; some of those places are maps,
and the atlas is reshuffled every league, so the tier of a given map changes.

Questions:

1. The full list of crafting-bench recipes that are unlocked in a MAP (any rank of the recipe): recipe text, rank,
   the map (or unique map) that holds it. Separate lists for recipes unlocked in the campaign (acts), in Delve, by
   unveiling (Betrayal), by Heist, in unique maps (e.g. multiple crafted modifiers, "prefixes / suffixes cannot be
   changed") and anywhere else. Source: the wiki's crafting-bench recipe list or poedb's.
2. For every map named in question 1: its tier on the atlas in each of the five leagues 3.25-3.29 (the base tier
   before atlas upgrades, as the wiki / poedb list it), or "not on the atlas" for a league where it was rotated
   out -- and in that case where the recipe went, if any source says. Then per recipe: minimum and maximum tier
   over the five leagues.
3. The Pantheon: for every major and minor god, which act boss grants the base power, and for every upgradeable
   power which map boss must be captured with a Divine Vessel (map name per upgrade). For each of those maps the
   same five-league tier table as in question 2.
4. Is there any statement from GGG (patch notes, forum posts, interviews) or a well-sourced community finding on
   whether recipe maps and Pantheon maps are deliberately kept at similar tiers when the atlas is reshuffled, or on
   what happens to a recipe when its map leaves the atlas? Quote it verbatim with URL, date and author.
5. One summary table at the end, two blocks (bench recipes; Pantheon upgrades): item | unlock place | tier range
   over 3.25-3.29 | always <= T10 (yes / no) | always <= T16 (yes / no) | notes. Do not interpret beyond filling the
   columns from the data above.

If a league's atlas list cannot be found, say which and fill the others. If the recipe list is large, keep every
row -- completeness matters more than brevity.
