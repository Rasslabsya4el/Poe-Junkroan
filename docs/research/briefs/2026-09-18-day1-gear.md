# Brief: what day-1 rare gear really is (Path of Exile 3.29, league start)

Report to `docs/research/2026-09-18-day1-gear.md`. Every answer needs a Reddit or forum URL with a
short quote, or a poewiki.net URL; "not found" is allowed. No advice, no build recommendations: facts
with sources, numbers where they exist.

Context: a build optimizer generates day-1 rare items for level 77-85 characters. Today it rolls
best-of-200 random rares per slot with PoB spawn weights and one bench craft. We want a deterministic
rule instead: chosen mod families with tiers that are typical for what a player has right after the
first two "stone" (pinnacle-guardian-tier?) bosses of the league, at yellow-map depth.

Questions:
1. Item level: which map tier is a league-start character typically running at the moment they kill the
   first two Eater/Exarch stone bosses (the "two stone bosses" milestone), and what monster level /
   item level do drops have there? Give the poewiki map tier -> area level table (T1..T16) and the
   rule for item level of drops vs area level.
2. What is the practical maximum mod tier reachable at that item level for common families: life on
   body armour, elemental resistance on rings/boots, movement speed on boots, attack speed / added
   physical on weapons, "+1 to level of all spell/…" on wands/sceptres/amulets (tier ilvl thresholds
   from the wiki or poedb, cite).
3. Community practice for PoB day-1/league-start gear: when people write "day 1 / day 2 / week 1"
   PoBs for a build guide, how do they set rare items — how many mods per item, which tiers (T1? T3?
   "mid rolls"?), and is there a convention like "3 useful mods + 1 craft"? Quote Reddit/forum posts
   from build guide authors or PoB-tutorial threads.
4. Bench crafting at day 1: which bench crafts are available to a character at that point (the
   unveiled/crafting-bench recipes acquired through the campaign and early maps), and which are not
   until later (multi-mod, high-tier crafts); cite the wiki's crafting bench page.
5. What counts as an unrealistic day-1 item in the community's eyes: examples people call out
   ("double +1 gems wand on day 1", "T1 life T1 triple res"), with quotes.
6. Anything on how fast gear improves from day 1 to day 3 / week 1 (what changes: ilvl, tiers, number
   of good mods), with quotes — for the later loadout rails.
